About

At Maths: MasterClass, Dr. Shamik Maharaj and his team strive to create an atmosphere of confidence and clarity for all students .

Our fully online, interactive lessons are held once a week for 1.5 hours per session. To ensure that every student receives individual attention, each class is limited to 10–15 students.

From September 2026 until the end of the academic year, every session is an Exam Preparation Bootcamp dedicated to past papers and proven examination techniques. By practicing these questions extensively, students develop the speed, accuracy, and confidence needed to perform under examination time pressure.

Every lesson is recorded and students may request access to the full library of all recordings.

For additional support, students may request exclusive access to our team of tutors (including Dr. Shamik Maharaj) via our WhatsApp helpline, which is always on. This ongoing support ensures that learning continues long after the weekly lesson has ended.
